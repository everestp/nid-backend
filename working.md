When you create your account on NID.xyz for the very first time, the entire process is **100% passwordless**. Here is exactly what happens step-by-step:

### Your First Time Creating a NID Account

1. **Visit NID.xyz:** You go to the homepage and click **"Get Started"** or **"Claim your .nid name"**.
2. **Choose Your Handle:** You type in the username you want (for example, `everest`) to claim your `everest.nid`.
3. **Connect Your Wallet:** Instead of typing an email and setting a password, the site asks you to **connect your blockchain wallet** (like Phantom for Solana or MetaMask for Ethereum).
4. **Sign to Prove Ownership:** Your wallet pops up asking you to sign a free, secure message (e.g., *"Sign this message to link your wallet to everest.nid"*). You click **Approve**.
5. **Account Created!** That cryptographic signature acts as your registration. Your wallet address is now permanently tied to `everest.nid`.

### What happens the next time you log in?

Because you didn't create a password the first time, you won't type one later either. Whenever a site asks you to "Sign in with NID," your wallet just pops up, you click sign, and you're in instantly.

**Zero passwords to remember, zero email verification links to click—just your wallet and your signature!**
